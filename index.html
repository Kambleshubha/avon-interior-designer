Thoroughly considered.

Notes from the studio

Ideas for a more
considered home.

Materials

How to create warmth in a neutral interior

Pair warm timber with textured linen and use lamps at different heights to soften the room after dark.

Furniture

The art of choosing fewer, better things

Start with the pieces you use every day. Measure the room and leave enough space to move comfortably around each piece.

Lighting

Designing a home around natural light

Watch where sunlight falls before placing desks, sofas or mirrors. Use sheer curtains where you want daylight with less glare.

Project checklist
