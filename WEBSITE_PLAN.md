# Interior Design Website Plan

## Goal

Create a premium, responsive interior-design studio website inspired by the supplied references without copying any one layout. The experience should feel architectural, calm, editorial, and conversion-focused.

## Visual Direction

- Palette: deep forest `#073F3A`, charcoal `#171A18`, warm ivory `#F4F0E8`, sand `#C9AA7A`, white `#FFFFFF`.
- Typography: elegant display serif for major statements; clean sans-serif for navigation, labels, and body copy.
- Photography: large editorial interior images, consistent warm-neutral grading, generous crops, rounded corners only where intentional.
- Layout: asymmetric editorial grids, strong whitespace, overlapping image/text compositions, full-width feature bands.
- Tone: premium but approachable; short, confident copy; clear calls to action.

1. Home

7. Journal / Blog
8. Contact / Book a Consultation
9. Privacy Policy and Terms

## Home Page Structure

1. Sticky header: logo, Home, About, Services, Projects, Process, Contact, primary CTA.
2. Hero: full-bleed interior image/video, eyebrow, strong headline, short promise, consultation CTA, project CTA.
3. Studio introduction: split editorial layout with copy and signature image.
4. Credibility metrics: projects, years, clients, satisfaction rate.
5. Featured projects: 3–6 cards with category, location, year, and hover reveal.
6. Services: four concise service cards with supporting imagery.
7. Signature project: immersive image-led story with key design decisions.
8. Process: Discover, Design, Develop, Deliver.
9. Testimonials: controlled slider; one story visible at a time.
10. Consultation CTA: strong contrasting band with short form or booking link.
11. Footer: navigation, contact details, social links, newsletter, legal links.

## Responsive Structure

- Desktop (1200px+): 12-column grid, max content width 1280–1440px.
- Tablet (768–1199px): 8-column grid; simplify overlaps and reduce display type.
- Mobile (320–767px): 4-column grid; single-column reading order; full-width CTAs; compact menu drawer.
- Keep tap targets at least 44×44px and body text at least 16px.

## Content Needed

- Brand name, logo, short positioning statement.
- 8–12 high-resolution project images with usage rights.
- 4 service descriptions.
- Verified statistics and 3–5 testimonials.
- Contact details, service locations, consultation link.
- SEO title/description and social sharing image.

## Build Phases

1. Confirm content, brand assets, sitemap, and success metric.
2. Establish tokens, typography, grid, buttons, cards, and forms.
3. Build semantic page sections mobile-first.
4. Add responsive imagery and performance optimizations.
5. Add restrained animation after layout is stable.
6. Test accessibility, keyboard navigation, browsers, forms, and Core Web Vitals.
7. Replace placeholders, complete SEO, analytics, and production deployment.

## Acceptance Checklist

- No horizontal overflow from 320px upward.
- Header, menu, sliders, forms, and CTAs work by keyboard.
- Images use descriptive alt text, dimensions, and lazy loading below the fold.
- Visible focus states and WCAG AA contrast.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected.
- Target Lighthouse scores: Performance 90+, Accessibility 95+, Best Practices 95+, SEO 95+.
