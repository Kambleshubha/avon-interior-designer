# Frontend Completion Checklist

- Semantic landmarks and correct heading hierarchy.
- No horizontal overflow at 320px.
- Keyboard-operable menu, modal, slider, and form.
- Visible focus and WCAG AA contrast.
- Responsive optimized images with reserved dimensions.
- Reduced-motion fallback for every animation.
- No unnecessary framework or large animation dependency.
- No console errors, broken links, placeholder copy, or invalid form states.
- Metadata, favicon, canonical URL, and accurate structured data.
- Lighthouse targets: 90+ performance and 95+ for accessibility, best practices, and SEO.
